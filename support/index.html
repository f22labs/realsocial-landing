Getting access

RealSocial v1 is invite-only. Invited agents receive a 6-digit access code from our team, enter it when the app first opens, and then create an account. Don't have a code? Email team@realsocial.me.

Connecting Facebook and Instagram

What you need. A Business or Creator Instagram account; personal accounts can't connect. To switch, open Instagram → Settings → Account type and tools → Switch to professional account. Posting to Facebook also needs a Facebook Page that you administer; Instagram does not.

Connecting. In the app, go to Profile → Connected accounts and follow the Meta prompts. Meta shows you every permission before you agree. If the Page you want isn't listed, check that you administer it and that you selected it in Meta's permission screen.

Disconnecting. Go to Profile → Connected accounts → Disconnect. We delete the stored connection and revoke our access token with Meta. You can also remove RealSocial from Facebook → Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites.

Connection stopped working. If you changed your Facebook password, removed RealSocial in Facebook, or Meta expired the connection, reconnect from Profile → Connected accounts.

How posts are created and scheduled

Answer a short quiz and RealSocial builds your week's content calendar. Review each post, edit or regenerate it, then approve. Approved posts are scheduled automatically and published to your connected Instagram and Facebook accounts at their set times, through Meta. You'll get a notification when a post goes live, or if one fails to publish.

Posts that did not publish

  • Check that your Facebook and Instagram accounts are still connected (see above). A disconnected account can't be published to.
  • Check that the post was approved. Posts awaiting your review are not published until you approve them.
  • Check that your Instagram account is still a Business or Creator account, and, for Facebook, that you still administer the Page.
  • If it still fails, email us the date and time of the post and which platform it was meant for.

Posts already published to your Page or Instagram account belong to those accounts. If you want one removed, delete it in Facebook or Instagram directly.

Signing in

Forgot your password. Use the password reset option on the sign-in screen.

Signed up with Apple. Sign in with the same Apple ID. If you chose to hide your email, Apple gives us a private relay address, so your account email may not match your own.

"This email can't be used right now." You will see this if you try to sign up with an email (or Apple ID) belonging to an account that was deleted and permanently removed. Sign-up is blocked for a cooling-off period after a permanent deletion; an Apple ID is blocked for 30 days. Email us if you need help.

Updating your license information

Go to Profile → MLS Connection → Update license number. Each license number can be linked to only one account. If yours says it's already registered, email team@realsocial.me.

Notifications

Turn notifications on or off with the Notifications switch on the Profile tab. If they stay off, also allow notifications for RealSocial in iOS Settings → Notifications.

Deleting your account or data

In the app, go to Profile → Account → Delete account. You can also disconnect only your social accounts, or email us if you can't get into the app. Full details, including what we keep, are on How to Delete Your Data.

Restoring a deleted account

After you delete your account it is held for 7 days. To restore it, sign in again yourself during those 7 days. We can't restore it any other way. After 7 days your data is permanently deleted and can't be recovered.

A restored account keeps its content, but your Facebook and Instagram connections are not restored, so you will need to reconnect them.

AI-generated content

RealSocial uses third-party AI services to help create captions, images and logo options. You review and approve content before it is published. If something generated looks wrong or inappropriate, don't approve it, and email us so we can look into it. See the Privacy Policy for what is shared with those services.
